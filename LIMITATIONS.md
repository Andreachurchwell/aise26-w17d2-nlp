# LIMITATIONS

This model was fine-tuned on a small subset of AG News (3,000 train / 800 eval) due to time and CPU-only hardware constraints, so results may not reflect full-dataset performance. The task was simplified to binary Sports vs Not Sports classification, which reduces realism compared to the original multi-class setup. Slice evaluation relies on heuristic rules (word-count buckets, keyword-based negation detection, and punctuation checks), so slice membership is approximate. The model can still fail on short or ambiguous headlines, negation-heavy phrasing, and cases where sports-related entities appear in non-sports contexts. Metrics are reported on a single sampled eval subset without statistical significance testing.
